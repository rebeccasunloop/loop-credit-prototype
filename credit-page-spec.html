Loop build spec

Credit — a dashboard tab for the line of credit

Date 2026-08-10 Requested by Rebecca Sun Environment next-app (~/Downloads/next-app-main) Status Awaiting review — not built
§1 · Segment-fit verdict
BUILD WITH CAVEAT — the credit limit is the strongest card-spend driver Loop has, but the application's decline path has no Self Serve exit.

Box 1 — can the product support the need? Yes, and most of it already exists in code. LineOfCredit, Fund, CreditApplication and CreditAssessment are real GraphQL types; /credit/apply is a live route; card limits, statements, autopay and a repayment wizard ship today under /cards. What does not exist is a place. Borrowing is currently scattered across three screens that do not reference each other.

Box 2 — can Loop monetize it? Yes, and through the mechanism LOOP-ICP.md §3 says actually counts. A credit limit raises card spend directly, and Loop's ICP spends that limit across a border — ad platforms, 3PLs, overseas suppliers — so the same feature raises card spend and FX volume, the two validation metrics in §2. This is not a convenience feature claiming revenue impact; the mechanism is spend.

The caveat, in two parts. First, LOOP-ICP.md §7.3: Self Serve is unattended by design, but a credit application ends in underwriting, and CreditApplicationStatusEnum includes DEAD ("Credit application was rejected") with no product-side next step. A Self Serve customer who is declined currently has nowhere to go — see §6, which is where that has to be designed or explicitly accepted. Second, Capital requires a personal guarantee for companies under $10M in gross profit (LOOP-ICP.md §6), which puts a step in the flow that only the guarantor can complete — it cannot be delegated to a controller or bookkeeper (§4).

§2

What the feature does

A Loop customer who borrows on their card today has to already know that "credit" is filed under three unrelated things: an Apply for Credit card that appears on Home and then disappears once used, their balance under Cards → Balances, and the payment wizard under Cards → Pay. Nothing in the sidebar says the word "credit," so the answer to "how much can I still spend, and when is it due" depends on remembering where to look. This feature gives borrowing one home in the sidebar: what the limit is, what has been drawn against it, what is due and when, and one way to pay it down — with the application flow sitting in the same place for customers who do not have a limit yet.

Primary user goal

See what I can borrow, what I owe, and pay it down — without leaving one screen.

Mercury's Financing tab, checked against Loop's ICP

The reference screenshot is Mercury's Financing page. Mercury sells to VC-backed US startups; Loop sells to Canadian businesses that move money across a border (LOOP-ICP.md §4). Three of Mercury's four tabs do not survive that translation.

Permission implications

Three real permission checks already exist and this page consumes them rather than adding any:

  • ability.can('view', 'capital', 'all') — sidebar visibility and page access.
  • ability.can('manage', 'cards', 'pay_card_balance') — the Pay balance action, via useCardPermissions() in src/components/pages/cards/hooks/use-card-permissions.ts.
  • canViewCardStatements / canViewCards — the statements table.

This feature has a two-role step, and it is not an approval. It is the personal guarantee. Capital requires one for companies under $10M gross profit (LOOP-ICP.md §6), and src/pages/guarantee_consent/ already implements it as its own flow with its own address fields and credit-report consent modal. The requester (a CFO, say) can submit the application; the guarantor (a named director) must personally complete /guarantee_consent. The Credit page must show the application as blocked-on-someone-else rather than blocked-on-you — see the Awaiting guarantor state in §6.

Dismissed

  • FX rate expired — cannot occur. Repayment settles a balance in its own currency; no rate is quoted and no quote can time out. (It would occur if a customer could pay a USD balance from a CAD account at a quoted rate — ⚠️ NEEDS: confirmation that cross-currency repayment is not supported.)
  • Cutoff time missed — cannot occur on this page. Repayment is scheduled against a due date, not a same-day wire cutoff.
  • Plan gated — cannot occur. Credit is gated by underwriting and the capital permission, not by plan tier. See §9.
  • Empty (no data at all) — folded into No line of credit; there is no state where a customer has a Fund and no figures.

Self Serve check

One state fails, and it is the one the verdict flagged. Declined (status === DEAD) has no product-side exit: no reason code is exposed on CreditApplication, no reapply-after date, no appeal. Its only exit today is "contact support," and LOOP-ICP.md §2 is explicit — any feature whose failure mode is "contact support" fails Self Serve by construction.

Two ways out, and the choice is a human's:

  • Design an exit. Show a reapply-eligible date and the one or two things that would change the answer — most plausibly connecting more sales channels, which connectSalesChannel copy already frames as raising the potential limit. Needs a reason code and a reapply window from underwriting.
  • Accept it. Declare the declined state attended-only, and accept that Self Serve customers who are declined go to support. Legitimate if the declined volume is small — but it should be a decision, not a default.

⚠️ NEEDS: decision — the declined-application exit. Owner: PM + Credit / underwriting. This is the one open item that could change the verdict from BUILD WITH CAVEAT to BUILD.

Two states improve Self Serve as specified. Limit reached explains a card decline in-product, which is otherwise a support ticket. Bank account disconnected names the blocker and links the fix instead of failing the payment silently.

§9

Plan gating

Basic $0 Loop Plus $79 Loop Power $299

Ungated by plan — all three tiers. Access is gated by two other things, both already implemented: the capital ability (hasLineOfCredit && manageLinesOfCredit, src/provider/ability-provider.tsx) and the underwriting outcome. No plan check appears anywhere in the credit code that was read.

This is the right call for the segment. Gating credit to Power would scope it to Key Accounts (LOOP-ICP.md §3) and cut off exactly the Self Serve customers whose card spend a limit is supposed to grow — a Self Serve account clears its floor at $50k/year in card spend, and a credit limit is the most direct lever on that number. Because it is ungated, §6 carries no plan-gated state, which is why that state is listed as dismissed rather than designed.

⚠️ ASSUMPTION: that no plan gate exists. Established by absence — no plan or subscription check appears in ability-provider.tsx, use-home-state.ts, credit-application-form.tsx or balances.tsx. Absence of a check in the frontend is not proof of absence in underwriting policy. Worth one confirmation.

Out of scope, open questions, assumptions

Out of scope

  • Capital for Invoices — invoice and PO financing (1.2% / 1.5% monthly, bankonloop.com/capital). Real product, strong ICP fit for manufacturers and retail brands with production runs, zero GraphQL model. Confirmed out of scope with the requester. Worth its own spec once the backend exists.
  • Venture debt and SAFEs — the other two Mercury tabs. Dropped on ICP grounds (§2); SAFEs additionally contradicts Loop's published equity-free positioning.
  • Distributing the limit across individual cards — bankonloop.com/capital sells this ("easily distribute funds across your cards") and CreditCardLimitSettings / CreditCardProcessorLimitSettings exist in the schema, but per-card limits are Cards' surface, not Credit's. Credit shows the account-level limit and links to Cards.
  • Changing the application form — CreditApplicationForm ships as-is. It has real issues (a hand-rolled <input> instead of the Input component, raw border-neutral-300 and text-red-600 instead of aliases, hand-built radios) but fixing them is loop-design-qa work on existing code, not this feature.
  • Credit limit increase requests — no mutation exists. The Limit reached state names the option in copy only.
  • Notifications — AccountLineOfCreditSettings.oneDayLeftPaymentReminder already exists and is configured under Settings → Notifications. Not duplicated here.

Open questions

  • ⚠️ NEEDS: placement decision — do /cards/balances and /cards/pay move under /credit, or does Credit link out? This spec builds the move. Owner: PM + Cards squad — it changes shipped routes.
  • ⚠️ NEEDS: decision — the declined-application exit (§6). Design a real next step, or accept the state as attended-only. Owner: PM + underwriting. This is the item that would clear the verdict caveat.
  • ⚠️ NEEDS: decision — the nav item's visibility gate. capital requires hasLineOfCredit, so an applicant never sees the tab, and applying stays a Home-block behaviour. Either broaden the gate to "eligible to apply" or accept that Credit only appears post-approval. Owner: PM + platform.
  • ⚠️ NEEDS: backend confirmation — is Fund.paymentSchedule a single upcoming payment or a list? Determines whether the right-hand card is a table or a two-row summary (§5). Owner: backend.
  • ⚠️ NEEDS: confirmation — can a customer repay a balance in one currency from an account in another? If yes, FX rate expired comes back off the dismissed list (§6). Owner: backend / payments.
  • ⚠️ NEEDS: compliance review — cost-of-credit disclosure on any surface that shows a rate, APR, fee or repayment total. Everything in §7 ships rate-free to avoid it. Owner: Compliance.
  • ⚠️ NEEDS: compliance review — adverse-action wording for the Declined state. Placeholder only. Owner: Compliance.
  • ⚠️ GAP — no loan-agreement document entity, so Mercury's Download loan agreement is not built. Owner: backend.
  • ⚠️ GAP — MoneyValue does not render in DM Mono, so number-tabular cannot be honoured without either changing the shared component or applying the class locally. System-wide; should not be fixed silently inside a feature. Owner: design system.

Assumptions

  • ⚠️ ASSUMPTION — scope is the card line of credit only, confirmed with the requester. Capital for Invoices deferred.
  • ⚠️ ASSUMPTION — Fund is the borrower-facing view of the same line of credit that LineOfCredit describes, and one account has one. Both types carry creditLimit, availableCredit and outstandingBalance; nothing read establishes whether an account can hold more than one. If it can, the Overview needs a selector this spec does not have.
  • ⚠️ ASSUMPTION — no plan gate on credit, established by absence of any plan check in the code read (§9).
  • ⚠️ ASSUMPTION — the personal-guarantee threshold ("under $10M in gross profit") is from LOOP-ICP.md §6, which is sourced from a 2025 segmentation document. That file's own footer says to check a threshold is current before leaning on it. Copy in §7 names the guarantee without naming the threshold, so a stale number cannot leak into the UI.
  • ⚠️ ASSUMPTION — next-app at ~/Downloads/next-app-main is current enough to spec against. It was not pulled or version-checked in this session. The loop-design-system review of 2026-08-07 pinned 9cdfd06.
